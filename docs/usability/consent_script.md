# Consent script (read aloud before anything else)

> "Thanks for helping. I'm testing the app, not you — if something is confusing, that is exactly what I
> need to find out, so please don't worry about getting anything 'wrong'.
>
> Here is what happens: you'll try three short tasks with the app, then fill in a standard ten-question
> questionnaire about how it felt to use, and answer three short questions about what was confusing.
> The whole thing takes about 25 minutes.
>
> What I record: which tasks you finished, roughly how long they took, any moments where you got stuck,
> your questionnaire answers, and your comments. I record you as a code like P01 — **no name, no
> contact details, and no photos of you**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the app itself works offline
> and never sends your photos away.
>
> **How the findings are used — please read this bit carefully.** This study is part of my assessed
> university project, so the results will appear in my report, which is submitted for marking, and in
> the project's public documentation on GitHub. What appears there is **anonymous aggregate findings**
> — counts, averages, how many people found each thing confusing — and possibly **short quotations
> from your comments, with no name and nothing that identifies you**. Your individual answers and the
> notes sheet stay with me and are not published. I will not record audio by default; if I think a
> recording would help, I will ask you separately and you can say no. If you would rather not be
> quoted at all, tell me and I will not quote you.
>
> You can stop at any time, skip any question, and ask me to delete your sheet and any recording
> afterwards — you don't need to give a reason, and you can ask me to remove a quote even after the
> session.
>
> Before we start: is it okay to go ahead? (verbal yes recorded as `consent_verbal: yes`)"

Then, verbally: are you 18 or over? Have you ever used this app before? Do you have any formal
training in plants or birds (for example a biology degree or a birding group)? Their answers go on the
intake sheet as yes/no flags only.

**Do not read the task list or the SUS in advance.** Give the app a 30-second open look first.
